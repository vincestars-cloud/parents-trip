Nine days, one thing a day.

Prices are for both of you. Every day has a rest built in. Tap “See the day” for the times.

Days 2, 3 and 10 are in Abu Dhabi, which the US government currently says to “reconsider”. The route can avoid the Middle East for about $280 more. The three choices.

Thursday 17. Leave home.

$60

The one thing: the 9:30 PM Etihad flight to Abu Dhabi, 14 hours, dinner and breakfast on board.

  • Leave Marietta at 6 PM. Ask for wheelchair help at the counter if you want it. It is free.
  • Aisle seats. Walk every two hours. Sleep when the lights go down.

Friday 18. Land, sleep.

$25

The one thing: a real bed. The airline pays for the hotel.

  • Land 7:20 PM. Passport stamp at the counter, free. Ask for the assistance lane.
  • Taxi from the rank at the door, $15 to $27.
  • Say “Etihad stopover” and your surname at the hotel. Bed.

Saturday 19. The white mosque.

$188

The one thing: Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque at 9 AM sharp, before the heat. Free. Golf carts for seniors.

  • Then indoors all day: the palace, an Emirati lunch, the Louvre museum.
  • It will be 100 to 105°F. Long loose clothes, a scarf for Stella, water in the bag.
  • Airport by 6:30 PM. Flight to Bangkok 9:10 PM.

Sunday 20. Bangkok. Sleep first.

$60

The one thing: nothing. The room is booked from Saturday night, so it is ready when you land at 6:45 AM.

  • Metered taxi from the Level 1 queue, $15 to $20 all in. Hotel by 9. Sleep till 1.
  • Lunch at Pier 21, the spotless $2-a-plate food court in the Terminal 21 mall.
  • Dinner at Cabbages & Condoms, a lantern garden with mild food. Bed by 9. No food after 7 PM.

Monday 21. Hospital, dentist.

$70

The one thing: the check-ups at Bumrungrad, one minute’s walk from the hotel. Like Emory, but done in one day.

  • 7 AM, fasting since last night. Blood first, then breakfast, then the rest. Results the same afternoon.
  • 2 PM the dental hospital across the road. Tooth prepared today, crown fitted Wednesday.
  • Dinner at Baan Khanitha, white tablecloths, air conditioning, everything can be made mild.

Tuesday 22. The palace and the birthday.

$178

The one thing: the Grand Palace at opening, then the giant reclining Buddha, then the ferry to Wat Arun. All within one square kilometre.

  • Shoulders and knees covered. Slip-on shoes. Anyone who says “the palace is closed today” is lying.
  • Foot massage in the temple pavilion. Out of the heat 2 to 4 in the museum.
  • Birthday dinner on the Supanniga rooftop facing Wat Arun as the lights come on. Mooncakes for the cake.

Wednesday 23. Crown on, fly north.

$110

The one thing: the 5:35 PM flight to Chiang Mai, 85 minutes, same airport.

  • 9 AM dentist: the crown is fitted. Collect the printed check-up report.
  • Leave the hotel 2:30 PM. Taxi $13 to $18.
  • In Chiang Mai, turn left in arrivals for the official taxi counter, $5. Dinner at the clean Anusarn food court.

Thursday 24. Elephants.

$210

The one thing: Elephant Nature Park, a rescue sanctuary. You walk beside them on a flat path and feed them. No riding, no shows. $77 each, pick-up at 7:30, back by 2:15.

  • Rest 2:15 to 4:30. The afternoon shower comes then anyway.
  • 4:30 the two great Old City temples in golden light, then dinner at Huen Phen, the northern-Thai house.
  • Rather see the mountain? Doi Suthep at 7 AM, cable car up (60 cents), khao soi at 11. $25 instead of $154.

Friday 25. A quiet morning, then home.

$40

The one thing: at 6:40 AM, give rice and fruit to the monks passing the Three Kings square. Then light a candle at Wat Chedi Luang for a first-trip wish. Under $5.

  • Late check-out 11:30. Car to the airport 11:20, $11.
  • Thai Airways 1:40 PM to Bangkok. Six hours there, then the 9 PM night flight to Abu Dhabi.

Saturday 26. Home.

$125

The one thing: six hours in a real bed at the hotel inside the Abu Dhabi terminal, 12:30 to 9 AM. The best $125 in the plan.

  • 9:20 AM flight to Atlanta, 16 hours, lands 4:30 PM the same day.
  • Open the free Mobile Passport Control app before landing. Home by 6:30.

Things to skip
  • Tuk-tuk rides. A photo in a parked one, yes.
  • Anyone saying a temple is closed, and any shop a driver takes you to.
  • The far-away floating market. Two hours each way in boat fumes.
  • Elephant shows and tiger selfies.
  • The river dinner cruise. Same view from the rooftop table, sitting still.
  • The cash machine’s offer to charge in dollars. Always choose baht.
