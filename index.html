The trip in one paragraph

You leave Atlanta on a Thursday evening and fly through the night to Abu Dhabi, a modern city in the desert. You sleep there one night (the airline pays for the hotel), see the famous white mosque and a museum the next day, then fly overnight again to Bangkok. Three days in Bangkok: Dad's dentist and both of your health check-ups on Monday, the palace and the river on Tuesday, then a short flight north to Chiang Mai, a calmer, cooler city of temples and mountains. Two nights there, then home — nine days door to door.
